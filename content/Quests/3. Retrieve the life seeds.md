## Overview
- After [[2. Clear the druids from the winery]], [[Davian Martikov]] revealed that he was the [[EoS 4 (Raven)|Raven Card]]. He says that the winery is dying because three of a powerful magic item called [[Life Seeds]] were stolen. He asked for our help to retrieve them in exchange for assistance later on.
- If we retrieve all three seeds, quote, "the lands of Barovia could feel [[Fortune 2 (Hope)|Hope]] for the first time in a century". An interesting choice of words given the Tarot reading possibly associated with the Ravens.

## Objectives 
- Clear [[Yester Hill]] and find one of the three seeds.
- Travel to [[Barez]] in search of the second seed. 
- Investigate leads to find the third seed, as [[Davian Martikov]] doesn't know where it is.
