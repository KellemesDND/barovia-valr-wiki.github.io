## Overview
- After attempting to enter Krezk when night was drawing close, the [[Dmitri Krezkov|Burgomaster of Krezk]] turned us down. Gonk convinced them that we could trade a favour in return of access to the city, to which the burgomaster told us that their wine supply has not showed up. We were sent to sleep at the [[Vistani Camp]] and told to go check out the [[Wizards of Wine Winery]] to see what has happened. Turns out, it was overrun by the [[Animal Bone Druids]].

## Objectives
-  Find out why the wine supply has stopped.
-  Fix the problem and bring wine back to [[Krezk]].
